    Closing the window keeps SoundCloud playing in the tray. On GNOME the tray needs an AppIndicator extension; without one, closing quits.

    Discord

    Shows the track you're playing on your Discord profile.

    Last.fm

    Uses your own Last.fm API account: create one, then paste its key and shared secret. The secret is kept in your macOS Keychain.

    Downloads new versions in the background and installs them when you quit.
